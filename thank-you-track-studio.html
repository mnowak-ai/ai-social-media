The Lyric Lab
🔒 Finish Station 3
⚖️ Remember the Golden Rules: AI assists — it doesn't replace you. If you use a starter line, you must make it yours before it locks in. Change words. Add your memory. That's not extra work — that's what makes it a real song.

Your three gold records are your raw material. For each one, write 1–2 lyric lines. Stuck? Tap "Give me a starter" — but you'll have to edit it before it counts.

All three lyric lines need the green LOCKED IN tag to continue.
