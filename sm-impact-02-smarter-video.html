Smarter video creation

Video used to be the slowest thing a social media team made. Captioning a two minute clip could eat an afternoon. Most of that work is now handled by tools, which means the job shifted from doing the tedious part to checking it.

What the tools do

Three things that used to take all day

Video AI is not one tool. It is a set of separate abilities that most editing apps have quietly absorbed over the past few years.

CAPABILITY 01

Captions, cleanup, and tracking

The tools that handle the parts of editing nobody ever enjoyed doing by hand.

  • 01
    Automatic captions. A subtitle track generated from the audio in under a minute, where typing it by hand would take close to an hour.
  • 02
    Background removal. Separating a person from whatever is behind them, without a green screen.
  • 03
    Motion tracking. Locking text or a graphic to something that moves through the frame so it follows along on its own.
CAPABILITY 02

Long video into short clips

Tools that watch a long recording and propose the moments worth pulling out of it.

  • 01
    Clip finding. Scanning a long recording for moments with high vocal energy or a clean start and end, then cutting them out as standalone pieces.
  • 02
    Effects and formatting. Reframing a wide video into a vertical one and adding the visual treatments that are common on a given platform.
CAPABILITY 03

Speech to text and filler cleanup

Tools that turn talking into editable text, then let you edit the video by editing the words.

  • 01
    Transcription. A full written version of everything said, which you can then search, quote, or repurpose into other formats.
  • 02
    Filler removal. Automatically finding and cutting every um, uh, and long silence across an entire recording at once.

The tool writes the captions in a minute. Reading them is still your job.

Activity 1 · Find five errors

Clean up the captions

Below is a real style of AI transcript from a short baking video. It reads fine at a glance, which is exactly the problem. Five things are wrong. Tap each one you find.

An AI transcript of a 60 second baking video. Five things are wrong. Tap each one.
Welcome back to Rosa's Bake Shop. Today we are making conchas, a Mexican sweet bread. You will need for cups of flour, too eggs, and a quarter cup of butter. My grandmother Guadeloupe taught me this when I was seven. The doe needs to rest for won hour before you shape it. Do not skip that part.

All five found. Every one was a name, a number, or a homophone. That is not a coincidence: those three categories are where auto captions fail most, and they are also the three that damage trust fastest when they go out wrong.

The limits

What these tools still cannot do

Knowing where a tool breaks is worth more than knowing every feature it has.

01
Be in the roomNo tool can capture a moment that nobody filmed. Someone still has to show up with a camera and get the shot.
02
Understand contextA clip pulled from the middle of a sentence can reverse what a person meant. Tools cut on sound, not on meaning.
03
Get names rightPersonal names, place names, and brand names are replaced with whatever sounds closest. Always read the captions before publishing.
04
Know what matters to youThe tool finds the loudest thirty seconds. It has no idea which thirty seconds actually support the point you are making.
05
Handle soft edgesHair, glasses, smoke, and anything semi transparent still confuse background removal. Plan your shot around this rather than fighting it after.
