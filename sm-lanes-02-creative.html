What this lane is

You are the one who builds it

Everything an audience actually sees came out of this lane. The video, the photo, the graphic, the words on screen, the sound. Other lanes decide what should exist and whether it worked. This lane makes it exist.

It is also the fastest lane to enter. Nobody has to give you permission to start. You can build a portfolio this month with a phone and free software, and a portfolio is the only thing hiring managers in this lane really look at.

What gets you hired

Five things that matter here

None of these require a degree. All of them can be built starting this week.

01
Taste you can explainAnyone can say they like something. Getting hired means saying why it works in a sentence your boss can repeat to their boss.
02
Speed on the basicsCutting a clean sixty second video should take you an hour, not a day. Speed is what turns a hobby into a job.
03
One tool, deeplyKnowing one editor completely beats knowing five badly. Employers ask what you can ship, not what you have opened.
04
Comfort being editedYour work will get changed. People who take notes well get hired again, and people who argue every note do not.
05
A portfolio, not a resumeNobody in this lane cares about your resume until they have seen the work. Twelve strong pieces beat a long list of claims.
