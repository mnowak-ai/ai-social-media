It is a real job. In fact it is thousands of real jobs. Every brand, team, hospital, college, restaurant, and city government you can name is paying someone to run their accounts.

Here is the part most people miss. The job is no longer just posting. It is deciding. And that is exactly the part AI cannot do for you.

The basics

So what is the social media industry, really?

Strip away the apps and the trends and it comes down to three things. Every single job in this field is some version of one of them.

01

Making things

Posts, photos, videos, captions, and graphics. Somebody has to think of the idea and then build it.

02

Talking to people

Replying to comments and messages, calming people down, answering questions, building a group that keeps coming back.

03

Reading the numbers

Figuring out what worked, what flopped, and what to do next week because of it.
